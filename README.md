# fomo-memebot — "The Big Treasure" research

Research repository: is there a big, sustainable, retail-executable edge behind the fomo (fomo.family) memecoin leaderboards?

**Read `docs/REPORT.md` first.** Short version: the leaderboards rank unrealized bags, following the leaders has no edge after costs, and the one candidate that looked like an edge (buying sharp dips in liquid, leaderboard-active tokens) was refuted by an adversarial audit (look-ahead liquidity filter, survivor universe, candle artifacts, understated costs). What survives is a small, capacity-limited 15-minute overreaction bounce that still has to be proven on a hindsight-free forward test. No big, sustainable, retail-executable edge was found; the report says so and documents why. Round 2 (report section 10) tested the viral "95% lose, buy the revenue protocols" thesis: following leaders into fresh launches only paid in the July launch wave, and PONS, the one real fee-earning token, is priced like pump.fun's PUMP on what it actually burns. Round 3 (section 11) quantified every money-making mechanism with its tail and costs; the only one open to a retail account with positive, measured expectancy is a delta-neutral funding carry on the mania tokens' Hyperliquid perps (CASHCAT: +16% on capital in 55 days, every two-week window positive). Round 4 (section 12) replayed every launch of Sep 3 from 5.9M v4 swaps and 419k bonding-curve trades and tested the "filter new coins and scalp" playbook on the whole universe: every filter and exit loses on both fit and holdout hours. Round 5 (section 13) priced the other side of those trades and audited it: creators took ≈ $562k in six hours from 185 sniper bots and organic buyers; serial launchers mostly sell to their own bundle wallets and their launching wallets net zero, but a one-off creator's seat is real (mean $137, median $14 per launch on a $100–240 stake, 99% non-negative). It is a launch-bot business bounded by the bots' appetite, not a trade. Round 6 (section 14) then measured the sniper bots themselves: the ones that buy in the first block after creation and sell within ten seconds are the winners (+29% a trade, $31k in six hours for the fastest), a rule computable at launch time reproduces it on every launch (+27% fit, +32% holdout, per-launch CIs above +20%), and the sensitivity runs show it is a pure latency race: half a second late loses. Round 7 (section 15) tested the second viral clip's three strategies on the same data plus an eight-week census of every stock-paired LONG launch valued today: "scalp the viral high" is negative net of costs on every variant even on survivors, the tokenized-stock jackpot is one token (AI) in 21,598 launches from the launchpad's first week, and "trench with a tracker" is the follower seat already measured three ways. Round 8 (section 16) priced the last unpriced seat, providing liquidity on the mania token's pool hedged with the perp: using the real in-range liquidity rather than TVL, a rehedged LP on CASHCAT/WETH loses more to the price path than it earns in fees plus funding on every period but the last fifteen days. Round 9 (section 17) scored the whole fomo app instead of the leaderboard: 11,738 wallets in one afternoon (net −$3.5M with unsold at zero), eight-week histories for the 251 best-looking ones (89% of their $1.07M realized came in the week of Aug 31; four of 251 consistent) and 120 random wallets (median $0). The winners swing-trade the liquid leaderboard memes with small clips and partial exits; random timing on their tokens does as well; the one rule-shaped behaviour (dip buying) fails hindsight-free. Round 10 (section 18) tested the influencer-catalyst scalp exactly as described (enter seconds after an influential poster's buy, stop −22%, sell into the crowd at +50%): on 276 fills with every pool swap for the next half hour it is about zero at bot latency, −6% at 15 s, −11% at app latency, and negative at any real size; entering before the poster pays +12%, which is the poster's own seat. Live shadow running.

## Reproducing

All scripts expect to run from a data root containing the raw pulls (`fapi/`, `rh/`, `helius/`, `gt/`, `dex/`, `prices/`); see `docs/ANALYSIS_GUIDE.md`. Keys (fomoapi `fapi_…`, Helius) are read from the scripts' constants; replace them with your own. Rate limits that matter: fomoapi free key 10k requests/month and 25 handle resolutions/month (never call `/v2/users/{handle}` in a loop), GeckoTerminal ~30 requests/minute, Robinhood Chain public RPC batches of ≤100 with a browser user agent.

Order: `pull_fapi.py` → `pull_sigs.py` + `pull_helius_parsed.py` → `pull_rh_logs.py` + `pull_rh_blocks_all.py` → `pull_dex.py` → `pull_gt.py` → `rh_ledger_v2.py`, `sol_ledger.py` → `dossier.py`, `classify_traders.py` → backtests.
